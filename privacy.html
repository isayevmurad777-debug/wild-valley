PawMed — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026
In short: PawMed does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics.

1. Information we collect

None. PawMed does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. There is no sign-up and no account.

2. Information you enter in the app

The app lets you record details about your pets and their medications — such as pet names, medication names, doses, schedules, and which doses have been given.

This information is stored locally in your device's browser storage. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or with anyone else. We have no access to it and no ability to view it.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is permanently deleted. You can export a backup file at any time from the app's Settings screen, and that file stays wherever you choose to save it.

3. Analytics and tracking

PawMed contains no analytics tools, no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no third-party trackers of any kind. We do not build user profiles and we do not track your activity.

4. Notifications

If you turn on dose reminders, the app may ask your device for permission to show notifications. These notifications are generated locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

5. Permissions

PawMed requests only the notification permission, and only if you enable reminders. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, or any other sensitive device feature.

6. Children's privacy

PawMed is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Since no data is collected at all, no data from children is collected either.

7. Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose personal data, because we do not collect any.

8. Hosting

The app is delivered as a web application hosted on Netlify. As with any website, the hosting provider may process standard technical request information (such as IP address) for the purpose of serving the page and preventing abuse. We do not have access to this data in an identifiable form and do not use it for any purpose.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
