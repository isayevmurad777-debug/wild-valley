Privacy Policy

Wild Valley Survival (com.wildvalley.survival) · Last updated: November 8, 2026

Wild Valley Survival ("the game") is a single-player survival game. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: we do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Information we collect

The game does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and does not contain advertising or analytics services.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device (in the browser storage used by the app). This data stays on your device. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game deletes it. The game also lets you export and import a save file; where that file goes is your choice.

Optional AI connection

The game has an optional feature that lets you enter the web address of your own AI server. It is off unless you enter an address yourself. If you do, a short summary of the current game state (such as tension and danger levels) is sent to the address you entered, and only to that address. We do not operate this server and do not receive this data.

Hosting

The game is loaded from our web host (Netlify). Like any web server, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify players.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
